Asset credits

Space textures

Earth day, night and cloud maps; Moon; Saturn and its rings; and the Milky Way star field by Solar System Scope, used under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. The published 2K texture files are unmodified. Their appearance is lit and shaded by the game.

These maps use NASA imagery and other source data with artistic adjustments. Planet sizes and positions in this fictional station view are composed for the game; they are not an orbital-scale simulation.

Deck material

Metal Plate by Poly Haven, used under CC0. The game uses the 1K color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps.

Typography and icons

Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Lucide icons use the ISC license.

Station geometry, instruments, interface and synthesized audio are authored in this project.
